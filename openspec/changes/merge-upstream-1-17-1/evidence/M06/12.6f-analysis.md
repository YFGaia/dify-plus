## Confirmed call chain

The three chart-backed endpoints are `daily-conversations`, `token-costs`, and `average-app-interactions`; `daily-terminals` is explicitly out of scope. They share a query model with only `start`/`end` in `api/controllers/console/app/workflow_statistic.py`. Console admission places the authenticated account/workspace in `RequestContext` (`api/controllers/console/flask_admission.py:67`). `api/services/workflow_statistic_query_service.py` passes tenant, app, and APP_RUN filters to the repository, but currently no account filter.

For `account=true`, derive the account only from `RequestContext.account_id`; never accept caller-supplied account identity. Add the parameterized predicate `from_account_id = account_id` alongside tenant/app/source filters in SQLAlchemy and Logstore. Equality excludes NULL historical rows. When account is absent or false, retain existing app-wide filtering and response behavior. Keep `daily-terminals` unchanged.
